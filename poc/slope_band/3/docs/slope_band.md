## 2. Anatomical and Algebraic Decomposition

### 2.1 The Rational Slope Advantage ($\theta \approx 26.565^\circ$ vs $45^\circ$)
A unit square tilted by $\theta$ has bounding extent $h(\theta) = \cos\theta + \sin\theta$.
- At $\theta = 45^\circ$: $h(\pi/4) = \sqrt{2} \approx 1.414214$.
- At $\theta = \arctan(1/2)$: $\cos\theta = 2/\sqrt{5}, \sin\theta = 1/\sqrt{5} \implies h(\theta) = 3/\sqrt{5} \approx 1.341641$.

This yields a **5.13% reduction in dilation waste** per square. Furthermore, a $1:2$ slope interfaces cleanly with $2 \times 1$ blocks of upright squares, matching Cartesian grid steps along the band tangent with minimum vernier distortion.

### 2.2 Quadrant Partition and Transposition Symmetry ($n = 908$)
The $n = 908$ packing is partitioned into:
1. **NW Pocket 1**: 100 tilted squares centered at $(\bar{x}_1, \bar{y}_1) \approx (8.3445, 24.9097)$, arranged in 13 parallel columns with profile:
   $$\mathbf{c} = (5, 7, 8, 8, 9, 8, 8, 9, 9, 8, 8, 8, 5) \quad (\sum c_i = 100).$$
2. **SE Pocket 2**: 100 tilted squares centered at $(\bar{x}_2, \bar{y}_2) \approx (21.9097, 10.3445)$, generated by applying:
   $$x_2 = y_1 - 3.0, \quad y_2 = x_1 + 2.0, \quad \theta_2 = -\theta_1.$$
   Across all 100 pairs, the symmetry residual satisfies:
   $$\max_{i \in [1, 100]} \left\| \begin{pmatrix} x_{2, i} \\ y_{2, i} \\ \theta_{2, i} \end{pmatrix} - \begin{pmatrix} y_{1, i} - 3.0 \\ x_{1, i} + 2.0 \\ -\theta_{1, i} \end{pmatrix} \right\| \le 1.25 \times 10^{-10}.$$
3. **Upright Cavity and Central Corridor**: 708 upright squares ($\theta = 0$) forming:
   - Complete upright staircase at SW: $x = 0.5 + i, y = 0.5 + j$.
   - Complete upright staircase at NE: $x = L - 0.5 - i, y = L - 0.5 - j$.
   - Unbroken solid diagonal corridor linking $(0, 0)$ to $(L, L)$.

## 3. Upright L-Border Composite Wrapping for $n = 968$

### 3.1 Composite Algebra
Let $P_{\text{core}}$ be the certified $n = 908$ packing with bounding box $[0, L_{\text{core}}]^2$ ($L_{\text{core}} = 30.63702956$).
To construct $n = 968$, we add 60 upright border squares ($t = 1$ thickness):
- **East Channel**: 30 squares placed at:
  $$\mathbf{r}_{\text{east}}(j) = (L_{\text{core}} + 0.5 + \text{margin},\; 0.5 + j), \quad j \in \{0, 1, \dots, 29\}.$$
- **North Channel**: 30 squares placed at:
  $$\mathbf{r}_{\text{north}}(i) = (0.5 + i,\; L_{\text{core}} + 0.5 + \text{margin}), \quad i \in \{0, 1, \dots, 29\}.$$

Total squares: $908 + 30 + 30 = 968$.

### 3.2 Proof of Separation (Separating Axis Theorem)
1. **Transverse Core-Border Separation**:
   For any square in $P_{\text{core}}$, $x \le L_{\text{core}} - 0.5 \cdot h(\theta) \le L_{\text{core}}$.
   For any square in East border, $x - 0.5 = L_{\text{core}} + \text{margin} > L_{\text{core}}$.
   Along normal $\mathbf{n} = (1, 0)$, the gap is:
   $$\text{gap}_x = (x_{\text{east}} - 0.5) - (x_{\text{core}} + h(\theta)) \ge \text{margin} > 0.$$
   Similarly, for North border squares along $\mathbf{n} = (0, 1)$, $\text{gap}_y \ge \text{margin} > 0$.
2. **Inter-Border Channel Separation**:
   The East channel spans $y \in [0.5, 29.5]$ at $x \approx 31.137$.
   The North channel spans $x \in [0.5, 29.5]$ at $y \approx 31.137$.
   The corner cell $(L, L)$ is empty.
   Along both $x$ and $y$, the channels are disjoint by more than $1.5$ units.
3. **Intra-Channel Separation**:
   Adjacent squares within East and North channels have exact unit spacing $\Delta = 1.0 \ge 1.0$.

Hence, **SAT clearance is strictly positive everywhere**.
