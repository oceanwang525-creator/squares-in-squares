## 2. Mathematical Formulation

### 2.1 The 4-Sign-Piece Epigraph for Variable-Slope Bands

Under SAT, the pairwise separation gap between square $i$ and square $j$ along an owner axis $a(\phi)$ is:
$$\text{Gap}(q_i, q_j, \theta_i, \theta_j; a) = (q_j - q_i) \cdot a - \frac{1}{2} - h(r) \ge \epsilon,$$
where relative angle $r = \theta_{\text{other}} - \phi$, and the support function is:
$$h(r) = \frac{1}{2}(|\cos r| + |\sin r|) = \max_{(s_1, s_2) \in \{-1, +1\}^2} \frac{1}{2}(s_1 \cos r + s_2 \sin r).$$

Each piece $h_{(s_1, s_2)}(r) = \frac{1}{2}(s_1 \cos r + s_2 \sin r)$ is smooth everywhere. The optimizer imposes all 4 linear constraints simultaneously:
$$\forall (s_1, s_2) \in \{-1, +1\}^2: \quad (q_j - q_i) \cdot a - \frac{1}{2} - h_{(s_1, s_2)}(r) \ge \epsilon.$$

Linearizing each piece around the current configuration $(q, \theta)$:
$$\Delta \text{Gap}_{(s_1, s_2)} = (\Delta q_j - \Delta q_i) \cdot a + (d \cdot a^\perp + h'_{(s_1, s_2)}) \Delta \theta_{\text{owner}} - h'_{(s_1, s_2)} \Delta \theta_{\text{other}}.$$

This establishes the convex epigraph around $r = 0$, explicitly penalizing relative tilts with an internal transverse separation cost of $0.5000 \cdot |\Delta r|$.

### 2.2 Heterogeneous Angular Degrees of Freedom

To avoid catastrophic boundary dilation, upright boundary and corridor squares must never rotate. The LP formulation introduces a boolean freedom mask:
- For squares with $\text{free\_mask}[i] = \text{True}$ (tilted band squares near $\theta_0 = 24.10^\circ$): 3 variables $(\Delta x_i, \Delta y_i, \Delta \theta_i)$ bounded by $[-\Delta_{\text{pos}}, \Delta_{\text{pos}}]^2 \times [-\Delta_{\theta}, \Delta_{\theta}]$.
- For squares with $\text{free\_mask}[i] = \text{False}$ (upright squares at $\theta = 0$): 2 variables $(\Delta x_i, \Delta y_i)$ with $\Delta \theta_i \equiv 0$.

## 3. Algorithm Architecture

1. **Initial Feasibility Repair:**  
   Solve a fixed-angle position LP with relaxation upper bound to eliminate any lattice placement overlaps.
2. **Two-Stage Continuous Descent:**  
   - **Stage A (Positional Relaxation):** Linear programming relaxation over $(\Delta x, \Delta y)$ holding $\theta$ constant.
   - **Stage B (4-Sign-Piece Angle Optimization):** Joint optimization over $(\Delta x, \Delta y, \Delta \theta)$ for all free squares, relaxing the variable-slope band into surrounding upright cavities.
3. **Geometric Backtracking & Non-Linear Validation:**  
   Line search with step length $\alpha \in \{1, 1/2, 1/4, \dots\}$ certified against non-linear SAT clearance ($\ge 5\times 10^{-9}$).
4. **Official Certification:**  
   Output formatted to the decimal-preserving Kaggle CSV specification and verified against the official interval metric and pandas 3.1.0 transport.
